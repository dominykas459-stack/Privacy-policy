Privacy Policy
App: Thrive Fit  |  Last updated: March 14, 2026

Welcome to Thrive Fit ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and associated services (collectively, the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

1. Information We Collect
a) Information You Provide Directly

Account information: email address and display name provided during registration.
Profile data: your fitness goals, personal motivation reasons, preferred workout days and times, language preference, and app settings.
Workout logs: dates and completion status of workouts you record in the App.
Custom content: any custom quotes or custom workouts you create within the App.
Problem reports: any feedback or bug reports you voluntarily submit.
b) Information Collected Automatically

Usage data: interactions with App features, screens viewed, and buttons tapped.
Device information: device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
Log data: error logs, crash reports, and performance data.
c) Notification Permissions

If you grant permission, we use your device's notification system to send you workout reminders at times you configure. We also send reminder emails to the address associated with your account. You may opt out at any time by adjusting your notification settings within the App or your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

Create and manage your account and user profile.
Provide core App features, including workout tracking, streak calculation, and motivational content.
Send workout reminder notifications and emails based on your configured schedule.
Display your stats on the in-app leaderboard (using only the display name you set).
Improve, personalize, and expand the App's features and content.
Respond to your feedback and support requests.
Monitor and analyse usage trends to improve user experience.
Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse.
Comply with applicable legal obligations.
3. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services to operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, authentication, email delivery). These providers only process your data on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
Leaderboard: Your chosen leaderboard display name and aggregate workout statistics (streak, total workouts, diamonds) are visible to other users of the App.
Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or the public.
Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or in-App notice of any such change.
4. Data Retention
We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. You may delete your account at any time from the Settings page within the App. Upon deletion, we will remove your profile data and workout history. Some residual data may remain in backup systems for a limited period consistent with our backup schedule, after which it will be purged.

5. Your Rights and Choices
Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data via the Settings page or by contacting us.
Deletion: Delete your account and associated data using the "Delete Account" option in Settings.
Portability: Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
Opt-out of notifications: Disable push notifications in your device settings or email reminders in the App.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed in Section 9.

6. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Security
We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services
The App relies on the following categories of third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

Cloud Infrastructure & Database: Secure storage of your profile and workout data.
Authentication: Secure sign-in management.
Email Delivery: Sending workout reminder emails.
Analytics: Aggregate, anonymised usage analysis to improve the App.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through the App.

9. International Data Transfers
Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your country of residence, where data protection laws may differ. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending you an in-App notification or email. Your continued use of the App after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
